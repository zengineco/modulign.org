**A Dimensional Address Grammar for Observable Reality**

Modulign™ (MGN™) is an open standard for classifying any observable phenomenon in the physical universe — at any scale, in any domain, at any time — using a structured address grammar with explicit epistemic, causal, jurisdictional, and evidentiary encoding.

It was built to organize a live-stream atlas of Earth. Its epistemological properties were an accidental discovery during formalization. That backstory is not a liability. It is independent confirmation of the architecture's structural necessity.

## What Modulign does

A Modulign address encodes:

- **What** was observed (domain, subdomain, activity state)
- **Where** it was observed (realm, geo hierarchy to four levels, node)
- **At what scale** it was observed (quantum to cosmological)
- **By whom** (observer type, domain competence, automated vs. human vs. hybrid)
- **When** (ISO 8601 timestamp)
- **Under what legal framework** (jurisdiction axis)
- **With what epistemic status** (^EVID, ^PROV, ^VFYD)
- **In what causal relationship** to other observations (Relation Registry)

A minimum valid address takes seconds to produce. A full ^EVID-grade classification satisfies legal admissibility requirements across all major legal systems without additional documentation.

## The key results

**ATNA — Address-Theoretic Non-Accidentality Principle**
A classification produced by the full Classification Decision Protocol (CDP) is non-accidentally true. The structural precondition for Gettier cases — logical independence of justification from truth — does not exist in the architecture.

**ATK — Address-Theoretic Knowledge Formulation**
A five-condition formulation of observational knowledge in which the justificatory procedure is constitutively world-engaged: every step of the CDP uses only input causally produced by the observed phenomenon.

**Synthetic Content Biconditional (Invariant I6)**
`REALM = VR ∧ MED = SYN ↔ SyntheticContent(ω) = TRUE`
This is not a tag. It is a structural biconditional in the grammar. Synthetic content has no physical locus and therefore cannot be assigned a physical realm. The VR/·:SYN combination is the only valid address for synthetic content — machine-readable, unforgeable by architectural constraint.

**Evidentiary Completeness Theorem (Theorem 6.1)**
Every ^EVID-grade classification satisfies Authentication (FRE 901(b)(9)), Chain of Custody, Best Evidence (FRE 1001–1008), and Expert Foundation (FRE 702/Daubert) as constitutive outputs of the CDP — not as post-hoc documentation.
